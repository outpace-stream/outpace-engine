# Outpace 1.2.1

For publication status and downloads, see the
[GitHub Release](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/releases/tag/v1.2.1).
These notes accompany the reviewed release commit; source documentation can precede publication.

Outpace 1.2.1 is a patch release. It fixes casting to DLNA/UPnP renderers so that a failed `play`
can no longer leave a TV playing a stream the engine has forgotten. It changes no defaults and
needs no configuration change.

## Fixes

- **A failed cast no longer leaves the TV playing**
  ([#497](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/issues/497),
  [#498](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/pull/498)). When `play` failed after the
  renderer could already have acted on it, for example when the TV did not answer in time, Outpace
  forgot the cast but sent no `Stop`, and the stream response the TV had already opened kept
  serving. Now:
  - a `play` failure that may have started playback is followed by one best-effort SOAP `Stop`
    (5-second timeout). Only a UPnP fault in answer to `SetAVTransportURI` is taken as a definite
    refusal and gets no `Stop`;
  - a stream response the TV has open ends when its cast is stopped, fails, or is replaced by a new
    `play` to the same TV, so a TV that ignores `Stop` stops receiving data.

  A failed `play` can take up to 5 seconds longer to answer. Only casting changes; it stays off
  unless `OUTPACE_ENABLE_CAST=1`. See
  [Casting](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/wiki/Casting).

## Distribution

Targets, packages, images and signer identity are unchanged from
[1.0.2](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/blob/main/docs/releases/1.0.2.md#distribution).
Images are published to `ghcr.io/outpace-stream/outpace-engine` (`1.2.1`, `1.2.1-minimal`,
`latest`, `minimal`).

## Upgrade and rollback

Upgrading from 1.2.0 needs no configuration change. Restart, then check `/healthz` and playback.
Keep the 1.2.0 binary or image for rollback.

## Validation and limits

The fix is covered by unit tests against a fake renderer (a non-fault `Play` error, a lost
`SetAVTransportURI` reply, a refused URI, replacement and the MIME retry) and by an HTTP test
checking that a real `/cast/s/{token}.ts` response ends after `stop`, which fails without the fix.
No hardware TV, multi-hour soak or hardware qualification was performed for this release. Preparation checks and candidate
CI are recorded in [release PR #500](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/pull/500). The
[release workflow](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/actions/runs/43737216784)
completed successfully on 2026-11-15. All ten required archives and six packages matched
`SHA256SUMS`, and their attestations verified with `--repo outpace-stream/outpace-engine` against
the `v1.2.1` tag. Both optional Android archives matched their separate checksums; they are not
attested. A third, `x86_64-linux-android`, was attached later from the `v1.2.1` tag by the
[Android backfill workflow](https://github.com/outpace-stream/outpace-engine/actions/runs/43771764456)
(#505); it matched its checksum, is not attested, and passed `/healthz` on an Android 15 x86_64
emulator. Standard and minimal image indexes contain linux/amd64, linux/arm64 and linux/arm/v7;
`latest` and `minimal` match their versioned digests, and both image attestations verify. The
downloaded Linux x86_64 binary and both images report `outpace 1.2.1`, and the minimal image passed
`/healthz` and `outpace healthcheck`. These are publication checks, not playback acceptance.
